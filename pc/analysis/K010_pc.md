## 2. Findings

**F1. Games are decided late, by economy.** In our losses the teams are level at R250-R500 against almost every
opponent, then we fall away:

| Losses vs | collected R500 | collected end | earned gold R500 | earned gold end | kills end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0xDEADBEEF (25) | 410 / 395 | 710 / 1055 | 1554 / 1523 | 3295 / 5328 | 4 / 6 |
| Borufeczki (20) | 323 / 383 | 752 / 1053 | 1063 / 1326 | 2692 / 4752 | 4 / 10 |
| QS2isLaw (15) | 457 / 305 | 1050 / 1179 | 1690 / 2072 | 4389 / 6078 | 8 / 8 |
| Shawn (7) | 412 / 412 | 964 / 1126 | 1841 / 2084 | 4426 / 5198 | 3 / 5 |

After R500 we collect ~300 more gems where they collect ~450-660, and our gold almost stops. In our wins the
opposite holds (e.g. Borufeczki wins: end 1057 / 785, gold 7307 / 6185). The early game is not the problem: we are
often ahead at R100-R250 in losses (QS2isLaw R500 457 / 305 and still lose).

**F2. Plan 4 is the likely cause of the late stall.** x1c1 plays plan 4 from R150: own half only (territory 0) and
aggression x0.65 for the rest of the game. When our half is farmed out and theirs is not, nothing sends us across.
Against 0xDEADBEEF (no fights: 4 / 6 kills) this turns 27% for x1 into 0/25. Locally the same shows: plan 4 wins vs
v88 (passive opponent) but loses vs x1 / v86h / v81hl, which keep farming the whole map.

**F3. Claudus Maximus hunts us; we feed.** 25 losses: our deaths 21 / game vs their 4; kills 3 / 17; gem drops 21 vs
4; we bank 136 / game vs 784; games run to R1405. Per champion: Cassi and Issac 5.7 deaths / game, Vesper 8.0,
Vendetta 4.2, all banking almost nothing. Plan 4's lower aggression does not stop the hunting; the champions still
walk into them carrying gems.

**F4. Results vs a team are deterministic per map.** Borufeczki: 01starvation, 00great_wall, adgato_gemcombat2 0-6
each; 00rows and 01gold_rush 6-0 each. So a ladder "record" is a handful of map outcomes repeated; those maps are in
local-benchmarks and can be reproduced against stand-ins.

**F5. Fuel is not the problem on the ladder.** x1c1 fuel-outs: 30 in the first 90 games (0.33 / game); opponents 233.

**F6. Every top team drafts the same core** (Heart of Ruin, Marcella, Vendetta, Vesper Vale, + Ryth or Tavi).

## 3. What I would do next (in order)

1. **Plan 4 with an end** (switch: X1_PLAN_END): plan 4 from R150 to R500, or until our half's available camp value
   drops below theirs, then normal x1 play. Test vs v86h / v81hl / x1 (the opponents that farm late) and v88.
2. **Late-game economy for x1 itself**: what x1 does R500+ on 0xDEADBEEF-type games (replay the Borufeczki 0-6 maps
   locally) and why gold stalls.
