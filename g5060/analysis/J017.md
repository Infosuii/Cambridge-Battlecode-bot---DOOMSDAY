## 0. How to count these games

**77 of the 80 opponent-map groups have the same result in all their games, both sides** (only Tzunami 00galaxy /
00pilgrimage and SquidProxyLovers 00lost_world are mixed). Both bots are deterministic, so a group of 5 games on one
map is about one result, not five. "0-25 vs 0xDEADBEEF" is **0 of 5 maps**; "15-20 vs Borufeczki" is 4 maps won, 4
lost, 2 single games each way. The whole set is ~80 independent results, and every opponent's record is set by which
maps its group drew.

Records: first 90 (K010 B, 14:38-14:40): Claudus Maximus 0-25, 0xDEADBEEF 0-25, QS2isLaw 10-15, Tatakai 6-4, Tzunami
4-1. Next 90 (J017, 14:50-15:12): Borufeczki 15-20, Shawn 8-7, Funky monkeys 6-4, SquidProxyLovers 5-5, Tzunami 2-3,
HouseOfCoders 4-1, Nameless 1-4, German Empire Destroyers 3-2. Total 64-116.

## 1. When games are decided

Losses (116), mean gaps us minus them: R100 banked +6, collected +3, gold -20, deaths +0.2 (level); R250 banked -34,
collected -18, gold -136; R500 banked -103, collected -39, gold -449, deaths +0.9; at the end we are **still carrying
218 gems vs their 87**, with 12.4 deaths vs 5.1. Wins (64): carrying 72 vs 95 at the end, deaths 3.7 vs 9.0.
Games are even at R100 and split between R100 and R500; a loss ends with an unbanked bag or a death spiral.

## 2. How we lose: three modes (every loss group classified; table in section 6)

| mode | rule (first loss of the group) | losses | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **conversion** (collected, not banked) | finished carrying >= 200, or collected >= the opponent | **55** | 47% |
| **feeding** | >= 15 deaths | 32 | 28% |
| slow economy | collected < 80% of the opponent's | 25 | 22% |
| close race | none of the above | 4 | 3% |

**Conversion is the largest loss mode.** In 36 of 116 losses our collected-minus-lost gems were >= 1000, enough to
win if banked. Examples: Claudus Maximus adgato_gemcombat4 (match a4e5bc09, all 5 games): collected 1107, banked
210, carrying 884 when they reached 1036 at R636. 0xDEADBEEF adgato_gemcombat5 (0b7cfe2e, 5 games): collected 1093 vs
their 1046, banked 659 vs 1025. Borufeczki 00great_wall (6 games): collected 1370 vs 1163, banked 928 vs 1031. Tatakai
01shortcut (28143bf3): carrying 426 at the end. QS2isLaw 00rushbait_v2: collected 1155, banked 829 vs 1012.
**It reproduces locally:** x1 vs x2 on adgato-gemcombat5 (run x1e-cw-ladder-x2-g5060 on this machine): side A
collected 1092, banked 322; side B 854 / 203; by R250 collected 434 but banked 84. Also gemcombat3 A (1309 / 889,
lost) and gemcombat4 B (931 / 581, lost). The gem-only adgato maps (no gold, no monsters) are where it is worst.

**Feeding** is map-specific: Claudus 03one_gem (**62 deaths a game**, 1 kill, R2000, banked 80 vs 125), Borufeczki
01starvation (26 deaths, 8 kills, R2000 tiebreak, 0-6), Claudus 03ultrastarvation (16), Borufeczki 00ribs (18),
QS2isLaw 00lost_world (20), plus Funky monkeys 00corners / 02arena, Nameless 00vault / 03honeycomb, Shawn 00gemfight,
Tzunami 00pilgrimage / 00ribs, German Empire Destroyers 00fragment. Few-camp ("starvation") maps are where x1 keeps
walking into a stronger team.

**Slow economy**: 0xDEADBEEF 00anvil (collected 397 vs 1168, 13 deaths) and 00fragment (505 vs 1006), Claudus 03twisty
(418 vs 1189), 03showdown (363 vs 1109), QS2isLaw 02harvest (873 vs 1150); our end gold is typically half theirs.
