# What the rival capture totals separate

In the loss sample, QS2isLaw collected groups worth 6,890 nominal gold to our 5,840, and bentheboss 6,960 to our 5,860. 0xDEADBEEF collected groups worth 4,800 to our 5,130 despite leading in inferred actual camp gold in four of six losses. This separates a likely camp-value gap in some matches from a possible conversion or gold-multiplier gap in others. Vesper collected 4,129, 2,385 and 4,069 inferred camp gold in the three rival loss cohorts. Those are the gold gains attributed to her captures, including any base award, shared fractions and multipliers. The replay does not isolate the passive's causal impact.

Completed quiet stationary stretches of at least eight rounds totaled 183 versus 95 champion-rounds in our seven sampled QS2isLaw losses, 136 versus 27 in six bentheboss losses, and 155 versus 387 in six 0xDEADBEEF losses. Counts are ours first. Our qualifying loss stretches did not start within five tiles of a live camp. A stationary run is classified by nearby enemies at the start and excludes recorded attacks, casts, channels, fuel suspension and gold or gem gain. Unfinished stretches are right-censored. This provides descriptive support for testing travel interruptions, with no claim that each idle turn was a pathing bug.
