# EXP-154 — Carry the safe-route bound into the immediate retry

The ward-only decision-reserve cap kept the exact R637 cast and ward but the same Heart suspended on the following round and three more times. A turn that has just failed to resolve the route within its safe allowance is a concrete reason to bound the next attempt, even when there is no new ward. Continue using the same cap on the next round after an incomplete or declined bounded search. A complete search or a round without the search lets the retry expire. The turn can still take its normal actions and threat checks.

Retain the R637 same-input and same-action-prefix requirements, zero mixed-fixture events, the strict Cassi and Issac controls, all earlier Heart controls and the 320 paired development gate. Require reproducible release and all-off v92 before L0. The development fixtures and recorded R637 search are the sole evidence used to design the change.
