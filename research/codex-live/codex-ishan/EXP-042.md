# EXP-042 — Identify the movement caller and remaining fuel with one trace point

The charged turn trail changed the first development case into a dead turn and gave the second more than 54k spare fuel. The exact tail guard nevertheless reproduced both original native suspension rounds and champions. Start with the exact PG dash source and instrument successful moves alone. Record the caller, destination, engine round and remaining fuel in the two cases. Use matching native actor and ward/move coordinates to determine whether the original turn reproduced. A trace that changes the original input or movement does not establish the cause.

If both cases reproduce and the same caller consumes the remaining budget, design an exact optimization for that caller and verify it against the original replay and frozen controls. Otherwise use the observations separately and do not generalize across the two maps. The round and champion selectors are diagnostic only.
