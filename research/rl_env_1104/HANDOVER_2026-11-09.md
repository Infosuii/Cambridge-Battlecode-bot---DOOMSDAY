### 6.2 Fuel: what v113 changed and which part matters

- **Budget.** Each body-turn has 100,000 fuel (200,000 on the first play turn).
  - Running out suspends the bot mid-turn: it loses that turn and resumes the next round.
  - The engine logs fuel per turn in `team-<side>.fuel`, inside every match download and local replay bundle. The parser is `tools/rl_env_1104/ladder_fuel.py`.
- **Fuel-outs per game.**
  - Ladder, 8 Nov: v112 8.1, **v113 1.5**, v114 13.4.
  - Locally: v113-based builds 3.3–3.4, v114 10.9.
- **Why v92-lineage bots overrun.** Their fuel checks used `bc::fuel_remaining() + nn_spent`, adding back what the network had already spent this turn.
- **What v113 changed** (v112 → v113):
  1. **"Honest" fuel checks.** Every `(bc::fuel_remaining() + nn_spent)` became `bc::fuel_remaining()`, in bot.cpp, common.h, runtime_budget.h, economy.cpp, pressure.cpp, tactics.cpp, world.cpp, tsearch.cpp and nnrec.cpp.
  2. **CLFS 9 → 15**, johndoe's fuel-safety bits.
  3. **Staged network scoring** (nnrec.cpp, "v204"): camp scoring runs in six bounded stages over one frozen observation, with a reserve of max(need, 20000) + 5000.
  4. **`channel_retry.h`:** a cancelled recall or gem submission is not restarted on the same tile.
  5. **Banking in altar range** during the route-timeout window.
  6. **Explicit enemy altar-lock query**, trying every open altar first.
- **Split test on v114** (final53 panel, 636 paired games each, run `split-01`):

| change applied to v114 | score (v114: 371) | vs v114 | fuel-outs per game |
|---|---|---|---|
| honest checks only (`cl_patches honest`) | 349 | **-3.5pp [-8.3, +0.8]** (family-balanced -4.8) | 10.97 (v114: 10.85) |
| v113's non-fuel fixes only, items 4–6 (`cl_patches v113fixes`) | 373 | +0.3pp [-2.1, +2.7] | 10.92 |
| CLFS 11 / CLFS 15 only | — | -3.3pp / -5.5pp | 8–9 |

- **What this shows.** The honest checks don't reduce fuel-outs on their own, and they cost strength: v92's logic does less work once it sees the true remaining fuel. v113's fuel-out drop comes from the **staged scoring** (with CLFS 15).
  - Untested consequence: v113, or anything built on it (v117+), may gain by restoring the old checks everywhere **except nnrec.cpp**. See section 9, step 4.
- **Ishan's notes on v113** (`V115_FUEL_MERGE.md` on his branch). Two possible bugs, neither of which measured a gain when fixed:
  - The staged rewrite dropped v112's same-observation refresh of the enemy block.
  - A stage's cost estimate never decays, so one expensive measurement can lock a stage out for the rest of the game.
  - About 2.3 of v113's ~3 fuel-outs a game fall in rounds 0–200.

## 10. Pitfalls

- **Disk.** C: hit 100% on 9 Nov and killed two evaluations. It had 38 GB free at handover.
  - Each local game writes about 250 KB.
  - Delete a run's `work/` folder once its `report.json` exists, unless you need its replays.
- **Pairing scripts.** In a `run_eval` report, `sides` is the string `"A,B"`. To map `work/g<i>.json` files to cells, iterate `["A", "B"]`; iterating over the string gives wrong cells (the bug behind the -9.6pp misreport).
- **Python.** Run the tools with `python -I`. Under `-I`, `PYTHONIOENCODING` is ignored, so call `sys.stdout.reconfigure(encoding="utf-8")` before printing team names with emoji or CJK.
- **Line endings.** Sources from zips may use CRLF. `patch` needs LF, and `cl_patches.py v113fixes` normalises it.
- **Uploads.** In pinned mode an upload doesn't go live by itself. Several 9 Nov uploads were cancelled within a minute, at the same minute as a newer upload, so upload one version at a time and check its status before activating.
- **Long commands.** Run anything longer than about 2 minutes in the background. Ladder commands rate-limit, so don't loop them tightly.
