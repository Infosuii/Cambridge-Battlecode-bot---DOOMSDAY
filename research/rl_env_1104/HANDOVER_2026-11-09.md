## 5. Fuel (compute budget) findings

- **Budget and trace format.** Each body-turn has a 100,000-fuel budget (200k on the first play turn). Running out suspends the bot mid-turn: it loses that turn and resumes next round.
  - The engine's own per-turn fuel trace is `team-<side>.fuel` inside every match download and every local replay bundle.
  - Format notes and the parser are in `tools/rl_env_1104/ladder_fuel.py`.
- **Fuel-outs per game on the ladder** (`ladder_fuel.py`, our own games on 2026-11-08):
  - v112: 8.1 (88% of games have one).
  - **v113: 1.5** (34%).
  - v114: 13.4 (100%).
  - Locally (`eval_fuel.py`) v114 and v112 run out about 10 times a game, and 19% of their turns use over 90k. Older bots (v86h, x3*) run out about 1.3 times a game.
- **Why v112/v114 overrun.** v92's fuel checks used `bc::fuel_remaining() + nn_spent`: they added back what the neural network had already spent this turn, so v92 believed it had more fuel than it did.
- **v113's changes vs v112:**
  1. **Honest fuel checks.** Every `(bc::fuel_remaining() + nn_spent)` became `bc::fuel_remaining()` (bot.cpp, common.h, runtime_budget.h, economy.cpp, pressure.cpp, tactics.cpp, world.cpp, tsearch.cpp, nnrec.cpp).
  2. **CLFS 9 → 15.** johndoe's four fuel-safety parts in nnrec.cpp: 1 slow-block estimates, 2 scoring margin, 4 slower need decay, 8 warm-up/rebuild margins.
  3. **Staged network scoring** ("v204", nnrec.cpp). The camp scoring runs in six bounded stages with cost estimates and a reserve of max(need, 20000) + 5000. Stages share one frozen observation and may span turns.
  4. **`channel_retry.h`.** A recall or gem submission cancelled by the channel guard is not restarted on the same tile. On a later turn it restarts only if it is safe by the same threat model.
  5. **Banking fix.** With an altar in range, gems can be submitted even during the route-timeout window (`tribute_off_until`). v112 refused.
  6. **Altar locks.** The enemy lock is queried explicitly (`altar_lock_at` prefers ours when both teams lock). Every open altar is tried before attacking an enemy lock.
- **CLFS alone does not reproduce v113.** On v114 (final53 panel, 636 paired games each):
  - CLFS 11: **-3.3pp** [-9.8, +2.8].
  - CLFS 15: **-5.5pp** [-12.2, +1.0].
  - Fuel-outs fell only from 10.9 to 8–9 per game.
  - The benefit comes from changes 1 and 3.
