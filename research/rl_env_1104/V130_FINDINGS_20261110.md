## 1. Corner camping (`wait-respawn` at tiny home camps)

- **Ladder game:** v130 against FürFortnite on 03twisty, 10 Nov 00:20 UTC (match `…7201`).
  - Our champions stand beside the single 120-hp camps near our spawn, camp 62 at (88,1) and camp 64 at (82,9), for
    30–67 rounds at a time.
  - That is 15.3% of the team's alive champion-rounds.
- **Local games:** in all 12 of v130's local games on 03twisty (6 opponents × 2 sides, run `v130-01`) it is
  **14.5–24.5%**, mean 18.2%.
- **Debug-build trace (`M round id mode group`):**
  - 79% of those rounds are mode `wait-respawn` on groups 10 and 12. Over the game, `wait-respawn` is 28% of all our
    champion turns.
  - Group score = value / (participants × (approach + 3.24 × rounds to respawn + kill) + capture + 4). On twisty a
    respawning camp next to us still beats far groups.
- **Is it worth it?**
  - The waits earn the team's average rate: **0.068 gems per champion-round, against 0.069 elsewhere**.
  - **18% of the waiting (579 champion-rounds) is a second champion on a camp an ally already holds.** That duplicate
    is only valued at 15% (`senior_claims`), yet it still beats its other options.
  - `kUseWaits` shops while waiting only from 90 gold. The waiters carried 20–70 gold, and a shard costs 30.
- **Candidates** (`wait_patches.py`): A shop while waiting from 30 gold; B bank while waiting from 5 gems; C skip
  respawning solo groups an earlier claimant holds; and ABC. Run `v130w-01` (final53) and `v130w-new83-01`.

## 2. FürFortnite's primed recall

- **Pattern:** 11,293 of their turns end with Recall and 10,786 of their next turns start with Cancel channel. The
  typical turn is *cancel → move → recall*. It is the same on 04passageway.
- **Effect:**
  - They complete no more recalls than we do (49 against our 50 on twisty), but each one is a turn into its channel
    already.
  - Only stuns break channels, not damage, so a hit carrier can keep its recall and escape.
- **Their economy:** about 3× our banking (39 against 12 submissions on passageway), about 3× our item purchases (118
  against 33; mostly Bruiser and Jungle emblems), and about 50% more wards.
- **Candidate `CL_PRIME`** (`prime_patch.py`):
  - **End of turn:** start a recall unless on spawn or channelling.
  - **Turn start:** keep it only when v130 would go home now and is past walking distance (low health, already
    heading home, or gold to spend while not capturing); otherwise cancel. The channel guard still applies.
  - **Debug check:** 11,367 of 11,459 turns end with the recall, with no errors.
  - Runs `v130p-01` and `v130p-new83-01`: v130p, and v130pe (with the early shop).
