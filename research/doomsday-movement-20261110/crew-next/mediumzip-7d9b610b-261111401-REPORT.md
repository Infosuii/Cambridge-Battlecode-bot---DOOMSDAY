## Main findings

The fresh-random matchup is close: v306 scores 52.0%, with a map-cluster
95% interval of 44.0%-60.5%. This does not establish a reliable advantage
on random maps. v348 is 51.5% (43.0%-59.5%).

The major-objective family is the clearest weakness: v306 wins 2/40 and
v348 3/40, compared with v306's 25/40 open-economy, 32/40 mixed-forest,
24/40 channels and 21/40 mixed-terrain scores. These are descriptive
subgroups of 20 maps each, not separately validated policies.

In the v306 random cohort, uploaded p98 spends 115,250 gold by round 150
versus our 78,050 (48% more) and collects 34,493 late large-camp gems versus
our 26,102 (32% more). Our team has fewer deaths, 310 versus 677. These
are associations, not replay-proven causes of the outcome differences.

Fuel timing matters: p98 has 1,246 exhausted-fuel events, but 1,229 are
within the opening five rounds. After that it has 17 versus our 202.
Do not interpret its higher total as sustained late-game computation stalls.
Zero native bot faults were recorded for either team in every match.

Original ZIP, source, compiled binary, maps, release settings and match
replays are frozen. No bot changes were made for this comparison.
