## Investigation

- **Rotation — Retry required.** The normal scorer excludes large camps above its team-size estimate, and a later rule can reset the selected camp to one participant. The first repair kept the number of reachable healthy crew members and compared a second job during idle respawn time. It slightly increased early camp gems on the generated screen when combined, while banking fell and all six Standing Stones cells still had zero centre collections by r300. The trace showed paths of 30–36 steps to the centre when the first repair admitted at most 18. Extending reach only for a compact and healthy crew caused some later commitments, with zero r300 centre collections and a seven-game deficit in the small combined screen. A third trial used the previous claim as the anchor and let three healthy teammates evaluate the next job while someone covered the current reward. It won 56/72 generated and 21/48 published, with no r300 centre collections.
- **Routine banking — Retry required.** The at-altar override caused a seven-win screen deficit, with little change to first deposits. Several changed outcomes kept the same first bank and r300 economy. The follow-up preserves camp preparation when a deposit attempt fails during a respawn wait. It won 61/72 generated and 17/48 published with similar early deposits. Neither change moved into the selected source.
- **Cover and pressure — Conditionally useful.** The cover action activated in all six contact-heavy debug cases on Fan, Corners and Lines. It moves toward reachable friendly cover for one turn after retreating if the bot is still hurt and out of attack range. The test retains the existing spawn, sighting and escape gates for isolated pressure, adding a two-helper requirement before overriding a poor one-on-one estimate. The expanded pressure path did not activate in those six traces. The published screen had fewer deaths with the two changes together, and generated wins were down two.
- **Dense-turn budget — Conditionally useful as a component.** The earlier uniform reserve lost generated games. The revised arm uses the larger reserve only on boards with many camp entities, leaving movement/action headroom after neural inference. Fuel suspensions fell from 479 to 343 on the generated screen and 541 to 408 on the published screen. Isolated strength was within one win of CrewActive overall.

**Conditionally useful — v221BudgetCombat.** The first generated, independent generated and additional published suites scored 686/1,000, 778/1,000 and 249/420 against CrewActive's 682, 756 and 249. Total fuel suspensions fell from 8,152 to 6,937, with zero faults. The independent map-bootstrap p10/p90 were +0.1/+4.3 percentage points. Early camp and bank results were slightly lower on that suite despite better final banking. All 1,000 independent games matched the seed and draft. The legacy preservation gate passed. Homonoliths' median home capture improved from round 429 to 428 and its three slow deposits stayed at rounds 521–560. Islands quiet stalled turns rose from 1,098 to 1,352 despite three more wins across 18 games. In the 20 independent replay pairs, idle moved from 2.429% to 2.390% and no-action reversals from 1.005% to 1.075%. `v221-selected-build.json` records this comparison and its artifacts. Further packaging is on hold while team rotation and movement are repaired.

## Staging, shared routing and movement

- **Staging — Retry required.** The inherited staging code draws a five-tile ring and subtracts every live camp's attack area. A camp with five-tile reach can erase the entire destination. `v222StageRoute` derives the ring from each live camp's actual reach and releases an empty or unreachable route. The 8-cell incident screen completed without faults, with Standing Stones and Homonoliths outcomes unchanged. Islands lost one win with the same early economy in that cell. A follow-up aligns staging presence with the larger ring and blocks a failed route in both selectors.
- **Shared routing — Retry required.** `v222CrewRoute` publishes a short-lived, addressed assignment when a healthy existing camp crew can take a more productive camp while a collector stays. The normal banking, healing and combat branches keep priority. The release build took a centre camp in one of two Standing Stones conditions by r300, up from zero, and collected 97 gems in that cell against 78. The other side still missed the centre. Both sides of Lines changed outcomes in opposite directions, with worse deaths and slower banking in the losing side. Across all eight incident cells it tied the baseline at five wins with zero faults. A debug replay showed an order at round 40 and three teammates joining at round 41. That order aimed toward the enemy home camp outside the old two-tile spawn boundary. In other orders, one named teammate often failed to join before the order expired. `v222CrewSafe` extends the exclusion to attack reach and lets members adopt a fresh order over a provisional assignment.
- `v222CrewSafe` retained the one Standing Stones centre capture in four conditions and had 11 wins against 13 for the baseline over 16 incident cells. It resolved missing acknowledgements in some traces. `v223CrewTempo` compared rival spawn travel with crew travel and carried an assignment through distant sightings. It won 9/16 with zero centre captures on Standing Stones by r300. The unwanted early enemy-side order still appeared. The shared order mechanism works, but its selection and safety model require further work.
- **Contact reversals — Retry required.** `v222ContactRoute` checks the actual return tile after a retreat and avoids an immediate return into a losing fight with multiple enemies. It recorded 66 vetoes in eight debug games. In the eight paired release replays, near-enemy reversals fell from 4.43% to 3.46% while idle rose from 6.80% to 8.70%. Both arms won six with zero faults. The explicit second retreat in `v222ContactExit` worsened idle to 12.17% and near-enemy reversals to 6.38%, with four wins. Keep it out of subsequent builds.
- **Supported aggression — Retry required.** `v223TeamPressure` replaces an estimate that could credit the same ally twice with damage from the champions actually in range. It allows an in-place attack against a solo enemy even when the usual verdict is retreat, provided the supported attack can finish comfortably and the existing isolation checks pass. The added action was absent from all 12 debug traces. This comparison does not establish an aggression improvement.
- **Ordinary pathing — Retry required.** `v224DirectJobs` compares the remaining reward rate of a proven active route with the new choice, allows a safe solo detour during downtime, and separates occupied goal tiles from static obstacles. It reduced idle from 16.56% to 12.78% and quiet reversals from 0.44% to 0.28% in 12 incident conditions. Near-enemy reversals rose from 3.59% to 6.50%, early banked gems fell from 702 to 505, and wins fell from eight to six. The trace had 328 route retains and no detours. On Standing Stones it retained a route toward the other team's home camp. `v225DirectField` follows a safe cached shortest path until blocked or near the group. `v226RouteSignal` narrows retention to actual backtracking, respects enemy arrival and leaves the unproven detour out. The frozen independent map set for checking generalisation is `arena/maps_route_holdout_261110226` (20 maps, four per family, seed 261110226). Those maps are reserved for one confirmation rather than candidate tuning.
- On the 12 incident conditions, `v226RouteSignal` reduced idle from 16.56% to 8.66%, lifted r300 camp gems from 815 to 996 and r300 banked gems from 702 to 775. It won seven to eight. In the 80 untouched conditions it won 58 to 56 and reduced reversals from 1.786% to 1.728%, while idle rose from 3.077% to 3.193%, early banked gems fell from 20,971 to 20,140 and fuel suspensions rose from 216 to 248. These changes do not generalise to a large idle reduction.
- **Detour retention — Conditionally useful.** `v227RouteDetour` stays with a dynamic detour until it passes the blocking stretch, instead of reverting to a shorter static route that leads back toward it. Against `v226RouteSignal` in the same 12 incident cells it reduced reversals from 4.03% to 2.62%, with 8.99% idle and one more win. In a second untouched 80-condition comparison (seed 261110227), it tied `v221BudgetCombat` at 55 wins and reduced reversals from 1.690% to 1.596%. Three or more champions contributed to 79.0% of early large clears against 70.1%, but there were fewer such clears, they took longer, and idle rose from 2.927% to 3.293%. Both arms completed with zero faults.
- **Bridge crossings — Conditionally useful.** The previous route held for a dash even when a crossing had no free landing or the dash had already failed. It could also report a successful dash as no movement. `v228BridgeJobs` tightened the wait condition, chose the best advancing landing and returned success on a dash. The two Islands traces recorded 137 rejected holds, including at a repeated stall tile. The bridge-scoped `v230BridgeWait` lifted Islands r300 camp gems from 251 to 299 and wins from three to four in four cells. It won 20 versus 19 across 24 incident cells and raised total reversals from 3.90% to 5.37%, primarily in contact. `v231RouteFallback` additionally recovered 37 otherwise failed steps in eight debug replays, mostly on Homonoliths. It tied `v230BridgeWait` and slightly increased reversals across the 24 release cells. General contact motion remains unresolved.
- **Small-camp respawns — Required behaviour, competitive retry required.** `v232NoSmallWait` treats 1–30 gems as a small camp. Hand and learned picks reject it if arrival would require a positive wait. A champion already in range leaves if the timer is positive. The eight traces recorded 64 rejected picks. The 24 paired conditions had zero faults, fewer reversals (3.90% to 3.43%) and slightly less idle (13.22% to 12.78%). Wins fell from 19 to 15. Standing Stones and Islands gained early camp gems, while Corners' r300 banked gems fell from 189 to 24 with a smaller change in collection. Removing a waiting assignment had also bypassed its bank and shop options. `v234DepartBank` restores those useful trips, remembers the deposit objective across turns and releases it if it cannot act. `v235BraveCrew` builds on it with a guarded four-versus-one strike.
- **Banking after a clear — Conditionally useful.** `v234DepartBank` lifted r300 banked gems across the 24 conditions from 1,132 to 1,456, versus 1,361 for `v221BudgetCombat`. Corners recovered from 24 to 167, Fan rose from 598 to 779 and Islands dropped from 262 to 226. The arm won 16 versus 15 with zero faults. The bank trip persists until it stops progressing, completes, or runs out of the original time allowance. The `v235BraveCrew` trace recorded 200 starts of these trips and zero of its new squad attacks. That version won 17 against the original 19 and kept the same early banking total. Its 11.71% idle was below the original 13.22%, with higher overall reversals (4.34% against 3.90%).
- **Fake recall — Conditionally useful.** The attached teammate notes concern a different v221 build. `v233ReadRecall` ports the observed countdown rule independently. The pinned local fake-recall opponent restarted its channel at turn end. None of 5,455 fresh four-turn sightings were treated as passive, while real countdowns were observed and recognised. Release wins tied 5–5 over 12 paired cells and fuel suspensions rose from 71 to 86, with zero faults. The correction remains separate from the new farming and banking source.
- `v236RewardRoute` detects a quiet two-tile reversal while travelling to the same camp reward. It excludes the farther tile for the route attempt, then releases the assignment briefly if no other legal route can advance. `v237SupportedPush` allows an actual in-range teammate plus another able to take a step into range to support the attack. The existing escape checks and short pursuit limit still apply. The independent comparison is frozen at seed 261110237, with 20 new maps from five families.
- **Same-reward reversals — Conditionally useful.** In the exact debug replay that reproduced 21 same-reward reversals by one champion, the isolated movement patch in `v238RewardTrace` triggered six reroutes and reduced the reversals to 11, with zero faults. The same check is in `v236RewardRoute` and later candidates. It addresses a particular route failure while other sources of reversal remain.
- **Strict wait ban and broad deposit priority — Retry required.** `v237SupportedPush` triggered 16 attacks in 16 debug games, eight with at least three nearby teammates. Six attack episodes produced two enemy deaths and one allied death in the following five rounds. The 80 independent release conditions at seed 261110237 had zero faults, but wins fell from 61 to 38, early large clears fell from 2.96 to 2.46 per game, and early deaths rose from 214 to 264. Idle fell from 2.74% to 1.74% and reversals from 1.45% to 1.40%. A separate 72-condition development comparison isolated the losses. `v221BudgetCombat` won 59, the strict wait rule won 49, adding broad departure banking won 35, and the full strict build won 37. Forcing early travel from small camps and sending carriers away before the next crew job were costly on generated maps. `v239OpportunityWait` switches away only to a ready, reachable job with enough support and useful reward timing. It preserves committed deposits while limiting new trips to genuine downtime or a short trip the crew can afford.
- **Squad aggression — Retry required.** `v240PressAdvantage` broadens the support rule to a healthy pair and lets a larger group take a short setup step. It checks for immediately reachable enemy reinforcements, legal teammate steps, ready lethal burst and an exit. The new trace distinguishes attacks from attacks that overrode the ordinary retreat verdict.

- **v244ClusterSweeper — Conditionally useful.** The selected solo farmer took 154 jobs in 12 debug games. It won 88/120 on the fresh generated set against 90/120 for its parent. It sometimes left larger jobs before the crew arrived. The donor pocket plan remains separate while the availability of a spare champion is resolved.
- **v245DoomsdayBlend and v246CrewTempo — Retry required.** The first blend won 87/120 against v221's 88 on generated maps. In four exact loss replays it emitted 75 departure events. In one, the same champion chose camp 24 in rounds 187–188 and camp 10 in rounds 189 and 191 while the first anchor remained asleep. v246 limits switches to ready camps with available claimants, retains a progressing replacement, and limits deposit commitment to short trips. The replay probe cut departures to 35, retained the existing replacement three times and repaired one of the four exact losses. It also cut quiet stalls on Islands to 347. On an additional 100 independent cells it cut idle from 2.66% to 2.19% but won 63 against 71 for v221 and 74 for v243. The early large-camp participation and idle gains alone did not qualify that build.
- **v247CampWindow — Retry required.** A nearby unclaimed reward can replace a short-gem wait when collection and return fit before respawn, after preserving the scheduled deposit and shop opportunities. It gained two wins over v243 in 120 development cells, but none of 12 debug replays triggered the new decision. The gain does not establish a benefit from the detour. v254 adds ready harmless camps and compares time to the actual reward, so there is an alternative even before anyone else clears a camp.
- **Repair arms.** v248 isolates bridge handling and the shorter safe deposit retry, v249 limits the staging change to long-reach camps, and v250 deprioritises only the exact rejected first step for one round. v250 still tries that tile if no other advancing step succeeds. The 120-condition screen finished 81, 80 and 80 respectively against v221's 79, all with zero faults. v252 combines the bridge, bank, staging and conservative camp-window changes. v253 adds a four-body response to an actual retreat verdict, with two allies already able to hit, immediate burst checks and at most one step into attack range.


## Verified ladder-test build

- **v248BridgeBank — Accepted.** The isolated repair checks that a dash landing is available before waiting at a hole and counts a successful dash as progress. It retries a stalled quiet deposit sooner when carrying at least 30 gems. It won 779/1,000 and 250/420 against the incumbent's 778 and 249 with zero faults. The exact Islands check fell to 293 quiet stalls from 1,352, with Homonoliths unchanged at median round 428. The larger v252 staging and reward-only blend scored 777/1,000 and 246/420. The isolated staging and rejected-move arms remain available for replay-based repairs.
- **v256CampScout — Accepted.** The amended solo-work picker checks wait at arrival, uses nearby live camps a champion can clear safely, and holds a progressing detour when the original small wait would otherwise be reselected. It preserves a deposit or shop trip that fits the wait. The 24 debug matches contained eight actual diversions, four followed by collection of the chosen group's gems within the original wait window. It won 87/120 in the development screen, 788/1,000 in generated confirmation and 260/420 on the additional published maps, against 79, 778 and 249 for v221. Both large panels had zero faults on either side. The 80% map-block intervals for paired differences were −0.7 to +2.7 points and −0.7 to +6.2 points. The movement checks retained Homonoliths at 428 and cut Islands quiet stalls to 379. Across the first ten generated map IDs (100 paired conditions), early large clears with at least three distinct attackers rose from 73.1% to 77.9%, back-steps fell from 1.66% to 1.63%, and idle rose from 3.30% to 3.36%. Those replay measures are descriptive.
- **Aggression — Retry required.** v253 and v257 never selected the new strike in their debug probes. The v258 repair distinguishes a moving target's recent location from another enemy, and replaces an absolute kill-time limit with the local damage exchange. It recorded 68 failures of the support requirement and three rejects of unfavourable damage exchanges in the targeted cases. In 41 of those support failures, three or more allies were nearby but none was in range or one legal step from it. v259, based on the packaged source, tests distinct reachable firing positions within two safe steps, with reduced weight for support that needs travel. The action still limits closing distance and checks reinforcements and burst damage.
- **Ordinary reversals — Retry required.** v255 applies a one-turn blocked tile only to a quiet two-tile reversal on the same reward. If no other route works, it releases the claim for two rounds. Its two diagnostic games did not reproduce a new reroute, although the earlier v238 exact replay recorded six. The route and support experiments remain separate from the selected source.
