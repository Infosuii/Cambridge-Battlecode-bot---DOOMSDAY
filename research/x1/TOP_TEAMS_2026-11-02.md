## Two separate ways we lose

1. **Slow economy (most games).** Top teams earn about twice our gold by R500 (typical 1,000-2,000 vs 2,800-4,300)
   even in games with almost no kills either way (e.g. newer team: 0-0 kills, gold 2,164 vs 2,514, banked 399 vs
   1,032). They bank ~1,000 by R650-R700; we need ~R900. Camp farming rate is the gap, not fights.
2. **Collapse: we feed (9 of 35 games bank under 100).** We die 23-48 times with 0-8 kills (Univ: 0 kills, 34
   deaths; Mite: 3 kills, 37 deaths; DEADBEEF: 4 kills, 23 deaths). Each death gives them 100 gold and 60 rounds of
   our production. The bot keeps walking back into the same losing contact.

Our 3 wins are games where we got the kills (12-14 kills to 1-6 deaths).

## What this changes

- **Plan selector input:** our death count so far (e.g. ≥ 4 deaths by R150, or deaths ≫ kills) is a direct signal
  to switch to a defensive plan (4 turtle / 6 early-contact answer). Add it to the branch-farm row features.
- **Anti-feeding rule** (new, phase 6, behind a switch): after a champion dies, mark where it died and to whom; for
  N rounds avoid that zone unless allies are with it; if deaths keep rising, the team turtles. Fires only in games
  where we are being farmed, so local screens vs x2 (≈ 10 deaths a game) will barely see it: it needs a sparring
  opponent that farms us (Agent 2's early-contact stand-in, phase 8, moved earlier).
- **Economy:** measure x1's gold per round vs these numbers; the plan farm's "fast bank" / "full map" plans and the
  joint search's farming knobs are the main levers. Track gold@R250 and gold@R500 in every gate report.
- Local benchmarks never show either failure strongly: v88 does not farm us. The league needs opponents that do.
