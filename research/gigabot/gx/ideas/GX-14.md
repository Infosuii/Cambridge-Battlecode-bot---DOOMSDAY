# GX-14: Intercept a large enemy bag with local support

The fight scorer already values large bags on visible nearby targets. The ordinary hunt stage mostly follows the closest recent sighting, which can spend time on empty champions. Moving directly after a loaded enemy can also follow it into its pack.

After normal combat, project up to three legal steps from a visible carrier toward its nearest altar. Try a cut only for a healthy, lightly loaded hunter with a nearby healthy ally and a local numbers edge. Skip enemies already in the ordinary fight band and preserve the default hunt when no safe cut exists. Measure transfer gains and deaths as well as wins; no expensive search runs without a movement reserve.
