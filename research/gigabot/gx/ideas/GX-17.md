# GX-17: Recover when a respawn errand is rejected

The farming state may ask for a tribute while it waits for a camp to respawn. If the tribute routine rejects the trip (for example, an altar is closed), farming returns immediately. A champion standing on a camp spawn tile can then keep the group from returning.

When the route is closed or unreachable, keep staging for the respawn. After any declined tribute, clear the forced deposit flag and fall through to movement. Test this fallback alone and with the exact engine submission duration included in the round-trip estimate.
