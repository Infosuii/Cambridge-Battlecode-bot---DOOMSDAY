# GX-28: Use respawn wait to stand on a better attack tile

The waiting path is satisfied anywhere in its attack ring. A champion can spend its last few idle turns in range of several hostile camps even when an adjacent tile has an attack on one member and much less incoming strength.

On the final six wait rounds, compare adjacent legal tiles for attacks and worst-case incoming camp strength. Move once only for materially less exposure or to gain an attack without increasing exposure. Keep current claims and the existing bank, shop and capture decisions. Test camp damage and deaths separately from player fights.
