# GX-10: Reuse exact world observations

On the six fuel-heavy diagnostic maps, hot-zone rebuilding consumed up to about 24k fuel in recorded turns. A camp's reach is static. Cache the mask for the current live hazardous members of each awake group and reuse it until the set changes. Skip inactive groups without allocating a mask.

A second optimisation removes SDK health queries for hidden members of partially visible groups. The SDK documents camp health as visible-only. A diagnostic build verifies that the omitted queries fail on actual maps with walls, bushes and large groups before the release run. Keep visible member queries and group state updates intact.
