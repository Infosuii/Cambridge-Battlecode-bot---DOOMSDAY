# GX-04: Spend a home trip on a whole emblem

The fixed gold-only home trigger can fire before a champion can afford its next emblem. An early return for four 30-gold shards delays the first passive and can create another round trip after the fifth shard becomes affordable. Health retreats and purchases on spawn remain useful even below a milestone.

On a healthy, unthreatened champion farther than four steps from home, cache the exact gold needed for the next complete emblem under its existing build. Defer only a gold-only trip and only up to 60 extra gold. Add a reserve for consumables once the first emblem is complete. A purchase invalidates the cache; when already heading home, do not change course.

Fuel: no work until the existing gold trigger fires, one inventory read per purchase cycle, and no extra work on threatened or low-fuel turns. Measure first-emblem timing, trips, collected gold, deaths, banked gems and paired pool wins. If the economy start gets slower, try only the opening milestone or make the extra allowance depend on the walk back from spawn.
