# GX-13: Value usable gems when the race is almost over

Gold buys power only after another shop visit. Late in a close race, a far-away champion may never convert another gold pickup into an emblem. The fixed round-based opening bonus in the scorer does not use either team's score.

Once either team has banked 700 gems, reduce the gold portion of the camp score as the lead approaches the winning total, with a smaller reduction near our spawn. Preserve gold value on gated maps without open altar access. This changes camp selection only; the separate banking policy decides when to submit.
