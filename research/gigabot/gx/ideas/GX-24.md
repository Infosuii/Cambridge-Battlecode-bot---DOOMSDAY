# GX-24: Ward cover beside a scoring objective

Regular objective wards are compiled out in x3. The existing reactive and ahead-of-route wards remain in play. A carrier or capturer may have a spare ward beside a nearby bush that hides the enemy's approach.

At a capture or a loaded tribute, use an available ward to cover nearby fog or a recent approach before falling back to the current path. Choose within two tiles, avoid nearby allied wards and keep the champion on its existing move or channel. Test a bush-only variant and a broader variant on open approaches.
