# GX-40: Warn a linked carrier when its partner is in danger

There were 313 linked death incidents in 175 x3mf non-wins. In 35 incidents the bodies were at least six tiles apart and a victim held at least 25 gems. A carrier can be far from the fight that takes its bag.

Use a short-lived alarm in the wounded twin's own claim slot only when already disengaging. Publish on smaller maps where the alarm values cannot collide with camp ids or existing fight orders. The other twin may bank a substantial bag near an altar if safe, separated and free of an in-progress capture. Compare inactive, alarm-only, combat bank and combat-plus-neutral arms to separate the effect of the shared slot from the deposit. Measure linked incidents and lost bags as well as pool outcomes.
