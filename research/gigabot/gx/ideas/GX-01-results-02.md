# GX-01: Marginal detour

Charging only extra altar distance while carrying at least 24 gems improved on the fixed-charge variant, but it still scored 306/516 (−35, z = −2.56). Fuel-outs moved from 548 to 546 and faults remained zero. Collection and banked score still fell. The change did not reduce gems lost to champion kills; they rose from 172.7 to 174.6 per game. Leave the return-cost adjustment off. A more targeted version would need to distinguish the incremental exposure of an actual contested path from distance alone.
