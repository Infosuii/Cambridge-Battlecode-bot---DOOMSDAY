# GX-03: Opening purchase diagnosis

In 76 losses against v92, the first observed team emblem arrived at median round 131.5 against 112. The first bank went the other way: median 96.5 against 105.5 among games with a deposit. In wins, our first emblem was earlier than v92's (114.5 versus 126.5). The difference describes the games and does not establish a causal effect of buying sooner.

We reconstructed shop visits from the order, available gold and quantities in purchase events. In only nine of 175 losses did a prior lock or crystal purchase push a recorded early emblem buy below its next combination price. Across all 516 games only three such events involved a first emblem. A change to consumable order has a small addressable sample. Focus the next strength test on return timing and early income rather than withholding the consumables.
