# GX-18: Read the capture countdown for visible rewards

The stock scorer charges a full capture duration even when a champion has already made progress. The SDK exposes both the countdown and capture ownership whenever a camp group is visible.

First, preserve an uncontested, safe capture with three or fewer rounds left. Next, also use the actual remaining time in the shortlist and cost estimate, and lower the score of a reward an enemy is projected to claim before we can arrive. These reads are conditional on visible rewards, remaining fuel and distance. A separate diagnostic build observed 2,158 captures or waiting rewards on six maps, with no bot faults.
