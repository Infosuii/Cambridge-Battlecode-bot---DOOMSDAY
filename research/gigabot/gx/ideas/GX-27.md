# GX-27: Draft an in-field shopper on long farm routes

Tavi can spend camp gold without returning to spawn. The walking geometry audit found spawn-to-altar paths of 120 steps on road-of-riches and 109 on shortcut; those examples motivate measuring the route to actual jobs as well.

Retain the first three picks and keep at least two frontline bodies. Consider Tavi for a later support or caster slot only if most productive ground is connected and at least half the reachable camp value is beyond the selected walking distance from spawn. Start with 22 steps. Evaluate the observed roster changes and opening purchase pace before attributing a score change to the role.
