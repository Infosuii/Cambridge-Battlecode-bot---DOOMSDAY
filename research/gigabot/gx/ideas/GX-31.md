# GX-31: Distinguish emblem completion from ordinary gold

On 9 of 175 baseline losses an early consumable blocked a shop visit that could buy an emblem. A blanket shopping change or an early item override is a poor fit for that narrow event. Add a capped score bonus only to a camp that supplies the remaining gold for an opening emblem while the champion is near its shop route. Compare with the separate test that assigns the first shop visit to a safe, contested local capture owner already holding the purchase price.

Reuse the existing camp facts and add arithmetic to eligible early camp scoring. For the purchase case, only inspect opening plan items when a local owner is established. Check first-item timing, uninterrupted captures, item spend, fuel and pool outcomes.
