# GX-30: Equal-time safe camp entry

A shortest route can reach the attack ring on an exposed tile even when a different first step reaches an attack tile in the same time. During a calm approach with a known live target, try nearby legal attack angles only when the walk cost stays equal and the camp-danger mask improves. Retain normal fights, movement and capture ownership.

The activation arms share one executable, including the checks for targets and move legality. Record first divergence, early neutral damage, capture timing, deaths and fuel at the same pool cells. The extra work is a bounded local scan, with a fuel admission check.
