# Transferable mechanics

- [Ma, Li, Kumar and Koenig, lifelong multi-agent pickup and delivery](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10868): token passing gives a job one owner; task swaps help when a better positioned worker can take over. Camp respawns are release times. A cheap local claim lease is a closer fit than running a complete route solver on each champion; visible safety and team jobs still matter.
- [Olsson, Brown and Helf, central place effects in foraging](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0040580908000506): transport and predation costs can depend on the load. The first GX-01 result argues against a blanket penalty on distant camps. Charge the extra return exposure when a bag is already at risk and measure banking and collection separately.
- [Benner, Halite III postmortem](https://github.com/aidenbenner/halite3): separate collecting and returning roles and evaluate movement in the local context. CAMBC has fewer, more expensive units, so keeping a helper near a loaded carrier is only attractive at short range when a pursuer is present.
- [Lux AI Season 1 engine](https://github.com/Lux-AI-Challenge/Lux-Design-S1): resource movement and deposit are distinct actions in the unit economy. A CAMBC job is likewise incomplete until its reward becomes usable; a team's route plan should include time at both the camp and the altar.
- [No Thoughts Head Empty, Battlecode 2023](https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2023-no-thoughts.pdf): carrier policy handles difficult routes by switching resource locations, and the combat opening values central control. In CAMBC the candidate camp and return route should be judged together, but a route cost cannot erase the value of denying a central camp.
- [OpenAI Five](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.06680) and [Tencent's hierarchical macro model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07887): preserve a strong local controller while evaluating changes to item economy and team coordination separately. Local wins against our bot family are insufficient to establish ladder performance.
