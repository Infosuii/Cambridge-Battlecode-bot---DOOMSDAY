Reading:

1. **Economy first.** Winners have twice our equipment at R100, before anyone has died (median deaths by R100: 0
   and 0). They spend 26% more alive time attacking camps, earn 36% more gold per attack round, wait at dead
   camps 2.7× less and take 2.4× the gems from big camps. Every winner opens with Treasure; we never do.
2. **Then combat, as a consequence.** With less gear we lose the damage exchange: we take 51 enemy hp per contact
   round from them and deal 39. Positioning, cohesion, focus fire and behaviour once hurt are at parity (see
   `V55_BRAWL_FINDINGS.md`), but we spend more of our contact time hurt and die twice as often in contact, losing
   our bags.
3. **Conversion.** In the 37 losses where we had equipment parity at R500 (2100 vs 1890), we still made fewer gems:
   respawn waits 154 vs 38, big-capture gems 78 vs 132, banks 22 vs 27 of median size 22 vs 27. Gold is not the
   problem there; **gem production and banking cadence** are.
4. **Our wins are the mirror image.** We win when the opponent's economy is worse than ours, not by out-fighting
   stronger economies.

Loss-family detail:
* **Opening economy (42):** R100 equipment 120 vs 240, R250 480 vs 810, capture gold per champion R250 138 vs 236,
  respawn waits 175 vs 65, no deaths by R250 on either side.
* **Early contact spiral (33):** 6 deaths by R250 vs 1; 20 vs 5 per game; capture gold R250 120 vs 272.

## 3. What everyone else does right

**Drafts.** Winners' champions per game: Vesper 0.93, Heart of Ruin 0.75, Marcella 0.74, Vendetta 0.66, Ryth 0.60.
Ours: Vendetta 0.97, Varg 0.77, Marcella 0.68, Ren 0.68, Prism 0.32, Cassi/Issac 0.31, Vesper 0.16. Our own results
by pick (map-confounded): with Ryth 64% wins (without 43%), with Vesper 64% (without 45%), with Cassi/Issac 29%
(without 55%), with Prism 37% (without 54%). Opponents draft Vesper in 307 of 325 games.

**Items.** Winners' first three emblems per champion: treasure>jungle>bruiser (179 champions), treasure>jungle>
durability (104), treasure>durability>jungle (53). Ours: jungle>durability>power (412). Top teams' carriers stack
Bruiser emblems (Intercontinental Fish's Marcella had 22 Bruiser strength emblems at R500 on boss-battle).

**Vision.** Winners place about 287 wards per game against our 64 (roughly one per champion every 20 owner turns,
close to the 10-turn recharge limit); we only ward at captures/altars. Not yet tested causally.

**Logistics.** Winners hop with teleport crystals on fixed 3-tile shortcuts (concentrated-wealth, swirl,
gold-rush); they recall for 90% of spawn visits against our 75% (we walk home a quarter of the time) and arrive with
more gold to spend (152 vs 95).

**Team that beat us most: Velvet Tributers (rank 67, 36 wins).** Vesper, Vendetta, Marcella, Varg, Heart (19 games)
or the same without Marcella (13); treasure>jungle>power/durability/bruiser; R250 equipment 1170 vs our 660; capture
gold per champion R250 263 vs 168; 1 death vs our 4.5; wards 276 vs 41; banks of 37 gems vs our 22; camp-attack share
0.28 vs 0.24. Turbo Gold Clusters, Electric Tributers and Rogue Elk (ranks 134-221) follow the same template:
Ryth/Vesper/Vendetta/Marcella/Heart, Treasure first, Bruiser stacks, many wards, half our respawn waits.

**Top-25 references** (earlier `V54_WINNER_PLAYBOOKS.md`: Intercontinental Fish, Claudus Maximus, Mite): the same
draft core plus Cassi/Issac or Tavi, Treasure on nearly everyone, one heavily funded objective carrier, coordinated
gate kills with first banks of 148-581 gems on boss-battle.

## 4. What v54 already changes, and what it does not

v54 (now live) adds crew-job coordination (fixes the staging-clock bug that made big groups untakeable),
Treasure-first + Bruiser item milestones, a lead lock on sole-gem maps and teleport shortcuts. Locally it beats v53
by +149 to +187 mean gems. It does **not** change the draft, wards/vision, respawn waiting (only −10%), bank sizing,
or the brawl-map weakness linked to carrying a teleport crystal (hooks 10/24 vs 20/24 without one).

## 5. Priorities this evidence supports

1. Draft a Vesper-centred core (Vesper + Heart/Ryth/Marcella/Vendetta) **together with** controller support for it:
   Vesper as the funded gold earner (reward ownership), Treasure first, Bruiser stacks. A pick change alone failed
   before (forced Vesper 3/6 against v53); the item/ownership mechanism now exists in v54.
2. Opening economy to R100/R250: plan routes so champions cycle groups instead of waiting at dead camps; take big
   gem groups as crew jobs early; reach the first Treasure emblem by ~R80.
3. Gem production and banking: prefer gem-rich groups once gold milestones are met; bank bigger bags (~30-40) when
   safe instead of 22.
4. Vision: a ward programme on routes, contested camps and altars.
5. Fight selection by gear: estimate visible enemies' stats and avoid even fights while behind in equipment.
6. Teleport crystal: keep the shortcut gains without the brawl-map regression.
