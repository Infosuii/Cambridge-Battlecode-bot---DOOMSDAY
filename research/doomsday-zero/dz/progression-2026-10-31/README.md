## Implementation

- The SDK has eight shared bytes. Six immediate claims retain the low 42 bits;
  the high 22 bits now multiplex the existing urgent crew lease with dated own
  cash/project reports. Deterministic even-round publication holds each report
  over the intervening odd round, independent of actor order. Leases preempt it.
  Absolute round decoding rejects modulo aliases; records expire after `4*n+2`
  rounds. Quantised cash is an **as-of snapshot**, never current affordability.
- Fresh project intent plus a matching immediate claim reserves visible workers
  during travel and recovery. Missing reports, negative current claims and stale
  records cannot establish remote combat power or perpetual ownership.
- A resumable Power health/strength frontier compares purchases with and without
  the prospective payout. It uses actual growth/recipe metadata, known ingredient
  combinations, zero/low/high sampled-cash scenarios, and a minimum scenario gain.
  Future full-generation sequence quotes pay home/recovery, banking and respawn
  time. Partial catalogs, jobs above eight members, unknown/saturated budgets and
  expired reports retain raw payout value. The general crew executor still allows
  its existing larger encounters; only this equipment counterfactual is restricted.
- Projection reuses checked geometry only for identical handles, slots, ranges
  and incoming factors with nondecreasing HP/damage. It cannot weaken current
  action admission. Public intrinsic growth is cached per observed body handle;
  gold is read only for self or from voluntary dated self reports.
- Crew recipient bids use the larger of generic gold value and conditional extra
  future gem production, avoiding adding both estimates. The recipient can reprice
  a concrete investment at payout, retain a bounded receipt, and commit an actual
  affordable home/shop visit only after observed own gold/gem credit and conversion.
  The final guard rejects split/short credit below the modeled full award. Normal
  winning/deadline banking and current safety checks take priority.
- Planned shopping now refreshes actual shard/emblem counts after each purchase;
  automatic combination no longer silently invalidates the rest of a batch.

planned purchase is established by the retained traces; those paths need a targeted
native check. The full-credit guard was strengthened after 04 and compile-checked.
