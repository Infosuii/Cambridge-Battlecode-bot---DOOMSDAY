All 17 represented teams are profiled. These purposive records are not ladder win-rate estimates. The 12 snapshot-leading teams have multiple styles; Supaswag is overrepresented as an opponent (eight games). Opponent code was not inspected and submission versions are unknown. Hypotheses below are behavioural summaries, not recovered source code.
