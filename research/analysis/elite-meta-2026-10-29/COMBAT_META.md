# Combat: remove threats to productive cycles

Winning sides have a median 4 physical deaths versus 10 for losing sides in this purposive sample. That relationship has clear counterexamples: winners have more deaths on tangle, elkring, fracture, yellow_brick_road and rows. Kills can fund stats, steal a bag, delay a competitor or open access; deaths can be tolerable if another route finishes sooner. Raw K/D is not a sufficient objective.

Combat micro must respect the legal action grammar. Melee can move and basic attack; ranged movement and ordinary attacks share an allowance, but resets can restore it. Only one successful ability cast is permitted across both slots each turn. Unit-targeted attacks/spells require the appropriate visible target; ground effects differ. Timers decrement at turn ends and effects resolve at later starts. The ledger preserves actual order, slot, cursor and target coordinate, including blocked movement and fuel rollover.

## Important causal fights and counterexamples

Arena: Claudus takes first blood at R100 and has the R100 gold lead, but suffers 24 versus 2 deaths and loses 186–1001. The winning controller's sustained production matters more than winning that first isolated exchange. Packs repeats the lesson under a mirrored draft: Claudus kills at R272, loses its Heart at R273 and loses the game 188–1004.

Broken_curve: the decisive late carrier loss is the rank-one team's Vesper losing 251 gems at R759; Mite finishes at R776. However, Mite's lasting score lead began R234. The kill strengthens an existing lead, rather than creating the entire victory. Vault: meow's Marcella loses 347 at R267 before NEETCoders delivers large bags at R348–354. These are appropriate cases for visible carrier priority and protection.

Stalactites: a287-gem bag changes ownership at R434 and again at R508 before a287 bank at R546. Aggregate collection overstates unique map production. Targeting and survival around the transferred bag have immediate score value. Rows is the opposite control: the only kill takes zero gems, the eventual winner has more deaths and less gold, and it still wins by 23. That match should not motivate a combat-only fix.

## What can be inferred about micro

The raw ledgers permit exact reconstruction of casts, moves, resets, recalls and basic attacks. Direct-target classifications use the acting body's own engine-filtered view at the start of its turn and should be treated as coordinate-resolved events; ground-area effects may hit additional targets not captured by that classification. Basic-focus counts exclude ability hits and require two distinct actors attacking the same target. They are descriptive coordination measurements, not proof of a shared target-selection algorithm.

An attack-reset search should compare attack→reset→attack against movement-compatible alternatives, while remembering that spending the ability allowance prevents a dash or shield in the same turn. Vendetta's camp-monster kill reset can change the next legal sequence. Shielding Heart while retaining missing-health damage may dominate an unnecessary heal, but exact values and incoming threats must be evaluated. Do not infer a fixed retreat HP threshold from observed separation alone: enemy motion, shields, carried resources and route purpose are competing explanations.

## Proposed combat evaluator

Use personal visible entities, own resources/cooldowns, legal masks, recent sightings with age and compact communicated plans. Enumerate a small legal sequence set; evaluate enemy threat removed, camp progress, bag risk, access opened, escape tiles and next-turn safety. Enemy equipment and cooldowns beyond observation are uncertainty ranges. Put expensive search behind local contact and a fuel reserve. A cheap legal action and safe destination must remain available before deeper scoring.

For counterfactual work, first reproduce the original accepted action/state trajectory exactly. Then change one move/target/ability and retain both sides' legal-state response assumptions. This study has not run a combat branch experiment and therefore labels safer retreats, earlier banks and different target priorities as hypotheses. Historical exact-reproduction work exists for the supplied-v69 baseline, but does not prove a new elite counterfactual or a v71 fix.
