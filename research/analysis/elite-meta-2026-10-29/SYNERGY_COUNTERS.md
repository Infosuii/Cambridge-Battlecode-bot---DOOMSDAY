# Synergy and counters: what is identified

The complete co-occurrence matrix is in DRAFT_EVIDENCE.md; SYNERGY_COUNTS.csv and COUNTER_COUNTS.csv retain both sample size and winning-side count. Neither matrix is a causal matchup table. Two mirror champions in the same match contribute opposite outcomes, team identities repeat, and one map per match prevents estimating map-specific effects independently.

| Candidate interaction | Mechanistic basis | Supporting / contrary cases | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vesper plus an efficient clearing body | More valuable capture credit if the receiver does not delay the group | swirl Vesper 13455 gold/820 score; historical forced-funnel variants failed | Medium as role complement; low for mandatory funnel |

| Stun/lock pressure against a channel | Tribute survives damage but can be stopped by stun or a lock | Mechanically legal; exact lock and cast events preserved | High legality, unestimated win effect |
| Linked-body focus | Killing either Cassi/Issac removes both bags and turns | Current rules; both winning and losing duo cases | High mechanics, no intrinsic counter coefficient |

The highest-value counter is often against a job rather than a champion: deny the sole altar route, interrupt the scorer, force a return trip before a large camp, or prevent an equipment spike from producing another capture cycle. This requires the acting body's own vision or communicated information. Full-replay inventory and enemy cooldowns are not legal universal inputs.

A useful counter-model should first predict feasible visible threats and objective access. Only then add champion-pair terms. Pooling the small matrix into dozens of fitted coefficients would mostly memorise team and map identity. A later experiment can compare a mechanics-only draft score with one additional interaction at a time on untouched map families, clustering uncertainty at map/group level.
