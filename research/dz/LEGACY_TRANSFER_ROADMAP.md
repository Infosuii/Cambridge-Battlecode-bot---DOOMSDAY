## Repair policy

Keep the exact source, binary and replay of every package. A failed screen starts
a diagnosis of decisions, execution, dependencies and computation allocation.
Do not remove the package after one failed attempt. The current sequence already
found two implementation failures: the full shortlist consumed the detail-scan
clock, and an optional combat forecast starved actual execution. Both were
repaired with the gameplay package retained. Earlier map packages 05, 06, 09 and
10 remain frozen for comparison. Arrival-only and speculative prefetch failures
are evidence about those implementations, not proof that opening work is useless.

Additional computation must buy a named result: a complete opening dependency,
a better feasible job, an executable roster or a materially different combat
decision. Stop optional work before it prevents an action. Measure banked score,
gold/spending, complete cycles, deaths and runtime together; idle survival is not
an improvement. Implement coupled known repairs before the matched screen, then
repair the diagnosed failure without discarding the rest of the package.

Reference strength is distinguished: v76lab/v81hl have actual matched local
results and the retained elite replays establish their observed decisions.
The public-khiri/public-decrest sources supply transparent mechanisms; their
competitive strength has not been established here. Old learned policy branches
are excluded. The user's requested risk tolerance supersedes earlier one-fix
abandonment guidance and the obsolete prohibition on legacy logic reuse.

Selected releases, online submissions, remote branches, the 14 reserved map
trajectories and seeds 121–124 remain outside this development screen.
