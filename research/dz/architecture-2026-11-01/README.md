## Mechanism corrections

- Expensive discovery could consume the auction before its first bid. A bounded
  retained frontier now shares discovery fairly and requests cold monster fields.
- Optional equipment counterfactuals previously blocked already executable crew
  projects. Raw projects now bid immediately; unpriced equipment gains stay zero.
- Followers' assembly clocks omitted paid travel/current resource work. Acceptance
  and arrival now have separate finite bounds; repeated packets do not renew them.
- Casting masks in SDK4 are exactly Chebyshev range boards. Ordinary spell range
  uses that metadata directly; map-sized allocations are unnecessary.
- A bank admission budget stop was interpreted as unsafe and could recall a
  carrier beside a safe altar. Deferred admission now holds; checked carriers are
  served before speculative work. Same-turn certificates require unchanged
  position/bag and no HP loss.
- Dense harmless camps exhausted bank cut ordering before a free root was tried.
  The zero-cut travel lower bound is checked first. Harmless respawn deadlines
  apply only to the selected root/exit, and a separate future quote can pay to
  clear a bridge again after a longer opening.
- Neutral exposure now uses current bulk visibility and dated member samples.
  Current awake camps override old defeated samples; unseen empty cells expire
  at their own renewal boundary. This removes repeated per-member SDK queries.
- Recovery cannot renew a stalled siege forever. Only initial funded restocking
  or observed target damage pays for a fresh approach clock. A complete current
  zero-exposure scene already supplies a safe endpoint without an extra step.
