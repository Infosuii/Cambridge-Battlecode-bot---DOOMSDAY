Do not retune the whole opening, enable blanket defence, or repeat the early-bank profile on this evidence.

**Turning point, measured:** match `3c064cd0-7d23-4866-8899-e0f3807ab20c` versus another name on 00split: DOOMSDAY had earned 510 vs 697 gold at r100 and 730 vs 1,262 at r150, with no champion deaths through r150. Native group events show 11 vs 14 clears; first clears r27 vs r21. The engine-2 replay snapshot at r100 showed team A's body 0 alive at (8,21), 60/1,000 HP with eight carried gems; keep the replay context when diagnosing health retreats. DOOMSDAY banked earlier in this case (120 vs 165) and had more banked at r300 (203 vs 94), so it is not an example of late first banking. v92's related health-retreat and priority paths are `bots/x3/src/bot.cpp:4814` and `:8200` at `df2975cb`. This is a source hypothesis: the replay cannot identify the historical submission or reconstruct its private decision state, so the exact executed source branch is unverified.

**Turning point across games:** the early sustained gold marker appeared in 15/23 matched losses versus 8/23 wins ([control data](DISCOVERY_WIN_CONTROLS.json), same opponent, only 3 same map, 16 same side). The split failed confirmation, so do not treat an early round as a stable loss predictor. Median round-150 earned gold was 460 vs 652.5 in discovery but 450 vs 388 in confirmation. By r600 the medians favored the winners in both (2,163.5 vs 3,951.5; 1,722 vs 2,381). Use the result to narrow an experiment, not to optimize gold instead of wins.
