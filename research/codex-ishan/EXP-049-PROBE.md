# EXP-049 — Freeze the equal-distance attack approach opportunity test

For a healthy melee champion on an early camp approach, check the original legal downhill step and alternate steps at the same field distance. A qualifying alternate puts an already visible live member of the same group in basic attack range while the original step does not. Exclude visible enemies, hot-zone tiles, routes with unreachable fields, and turns where the alternate changes the committed job. This tests whether the same target can start taking damage earlier.

Log the original first step and the first qualifying equal-distance alternative without changing the action. Use 64 frozen development cases, both sides and both varied opponents. Require independent opportunities on at least two maps, both opponents represented, and zero faults or codes before changing production movement.
