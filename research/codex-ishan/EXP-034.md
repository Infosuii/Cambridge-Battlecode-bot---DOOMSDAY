# EXP-034 — Release a stationary opening approach with no route field

In the direct rival review, DOOMSDAY spent 183 and 136 champion-rounds in long quiet stationary stretches in sampled losses to QS2isLaw and bentheboss respectively, against 95 and 27 for the rivals. The observation excludes spawn, recorded fuel suspensions, combat or channel actions, gains in gold or gems, and enemy proximity at the start. None of our long loss stretches in the three cohorts began within five tiles of a live camp. This is a descriptive pattern with unequal sample sizes, not a direct diagnosis of historical bot state.

The archived PG rule requires a built group distance field. Probe the distinct case where a healthy champion stays on the same tile while approaching the same solo camp for eight consecutive opening rounds, has no group field, is out of enemy view and outside awake-camp danger. Keep the existing cutoff-specific approach handling. In the diagnostic only, report the first qualifying decision per run without changing the target or action. Require two independent development maps, both opponents, no faults and valid logs. A release would hold off that camp for the existing PG 40 rounds and return through the normal farm fallback for this one turn, with no extra scoring. It must reduce the stalled-approach behavior in native dev and pass the original L0–L2 gates.
