# Discovery 7 follow-up cards

## EXP-092 — Measure the original field-order cost at R1

The isolated field-order gate removed 15 early suspensions and two cells increased later. Sample the original entry and exit of `World::prepare_field_order` on the two development maps where at least five actors suspended in round one. Distinguish a suspension inside field ordering from work that crosses after it. Preserve native actor and round. Only a measured cost or hot operation supports an exact replacement.

## EXP-093 — Use the approach that ends on a reward tile

EXP-086 found that only five of 36 earlier clears yielded earlier payouts. Probe the normal planned approach to a selected low-health last camp when two equal-time legal first steps are available. One step must land on the group's reward tiles without increasing camp damage exposure or range to the target. Require earlier native payout on two independently measured maps against both varied opponents before a release.

## EXP-094 — Trace the first damaging trade before a camp exit

Cuts had no opening fuel suspension and showed a large health difference in the same-role turn. Record the first loss of 25% max HP while at a live camp, then the next own-view retreat opportunity in earlier independent losses. Compare against successful clears under similar local threats. Choose an action only where the alternative is legal in the replayed state and preserves team collection.

## EXP-095 — Measure help claims that arrive too late for the current clear

The sampled winners continued to collect more by R300 while the earlier home-side and extra-collector changes failed. Look for a group already on pace to clear before a second ally arrives, when that ally has another visible safe objective. Test a release only if the decision repeats on two maps and both opponents and the native alternative improves total team collection.

## EXP-096 — Check for an immediate nearby reward after a matched clear

EXP-048 found no safe detour during respawn waits. This separate probe starts at the original post-clear decision. Check whether a different already-cleared reward can be collected before the current group's guaranteed claim, with visible state, bounded travel and a teammate demonstrably retaining the current claim. Preserve the original route when either payout cannot be verified.
