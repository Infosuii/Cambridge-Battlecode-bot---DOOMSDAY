# EXP-004: Guard proactive ward refresh in CPG

**Evidence:** winners placed more wards in 12/16 discovery and 5/8 confirmation games ([discovery](LOSS_DISCOVERY.json), [confirmation](LOSS_CONFIRM.json)). That supports preserving ward placement, without inferring that adding wards wins. The earlier blanket refresh experiment lost about 10 games (`research/x3/MAP_IDEA_FINDINGS.md:85`). The JD development fuel profile (`12c1804b:research/jd-g5070-1104/FUEL_BENCH.md`) observed second-world-update suspensions and a 53,778-fuel vision-phase maximum, with a 6k tail reserve. Frozen CPG failed the strict safety gate despite lowering total suspensions.

**Hypothesis:** `ward_reveal()` currently returns true for successful proactive `ward_ahead()` even with no fresh hit or lost sighting; the caller then rebuilds the whole observation before choosing an action. Keep the placement but defer that refresh to normal observation next turn if fewer than 60k fuel remain. Immediate reactive reveal, ambush handling and all other play are unchanged. This targets a specific excess-cost path rather than reintroducing blanket vision changes. CPG source: `cb8d0105`, `bots/jd-g5070-resume-0715/src/bot.cpp` (local verified archive's lines 2453–2475 and 8551).
