# Discovery 5 follow-up cards

## EXP-047 — Charge for camp reward split before a second arrival

The reserved winner collected more gold from a similar number of captures. Probe turns before R150 where two healthy allies independently move to a group that one could clear and the later arrival would dilute the capture reward without shortening completion. Require the existing claims, observed health and route distances to support the alternative, and count actual differing first targets across at least two maps and both varied opponents. The proposed change should improve team camp gems and gold with the draft and recall decisions held fixed.

## EXP-048 — Use a cleared payout while the committed camp is waiting to respawn

Probe safe turns where v92 retains a respawning job but a separate nearby payout is already available, reachable and unclaimed. Compare the complete detour with the remaining wait before choosing it. Require independent cases on at least two development maps, a clear ownership signal, no loss of the original spawn opportunity and a measurable change in early team collection. This is an opportunistic capture of an already-cleared payout.

## EXP-049 — Prefer the approach that reveals attackable camps earlier

The Front and Gold Rush show how much visible camp state can differ at the same round and role. In a development trace, compare equal-length routes to the same job where one moves toward an actually visible safe attack tile one round earlier. Preserve the chosen job, avoid unobserved monsters as score inputs, and verify the action and collection times on at least two maps with a charged fixture. Do not infer historical missed attacks from visibility alone.

## EXP-050 — Probe pre-recall pickup when a safe clear is already one action away

Shop timing varied even in the same champion role at the round-150 turning points. Probe existing recall decisions with a safe, already visible and in-range clear available that turn, using `can_attack`, target HP and a valid release action. Compare one clear before the same recall with the original recall, preserving the previous shop destination and never adding a trip. Require a measured early reward improvement on two maps without a late purchase at the next planned visit.

## EXP-051 — Test conversion only when extra collection does not explain the bank deficit

Gemfight shows that loss while carrying can matter in individual games. The pattern where DOOMSDAY collected at least as much as the winner and still banked less repeated in only one of eight reserved cases. The five-of-eight discovery gate closed this broad conversion change. Leave the default tribute policy in place unless a separately defined visible tactical condition clears a fresh confirmation gate.
