# EXP-031 — Defer first field ordering when a field cannot fit

On deterministic turns v92 prepares the full group order even when its own fuel admission would disallow the first field. In that case the prepared order has no consumers on the current turn. Measure the number of such deferrals on the 90 frozen development maps against x3inv, both sides, and check actual field-order spend in a charged prototype. Record when no field can start and when the retained order is later built. Require at least two maps and no additional diagnostic faults before a release.

Use the existing room predicate after the altar and enemy field stages. If it fails and the order is still empty, wait until a later turn; the existing fallback handles absent group fields. Reuse the same sort, spawn-field distances, field quota and reserve. Keep the deterministic order and avoid extra work after it has been built. This is a v92 runtime change, separate from CPG's whole-turn governor and distinct from the siege sequence. Prediction is fewer early suspensions and eventual field availability. Require a measured whole-game safety improvement on the unchanged pool and every original L0 gate before proceeding.
