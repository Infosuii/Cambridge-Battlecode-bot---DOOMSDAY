# EXP-021 — Bank or shelter loaded carriers before a second exposed move

The third Discovery sample shows more early banking and more late enemy clears. In one selected replay our Vendetta died carrying 43 gems at R71. The early-death and no-PvP-action predicates failed, so general fight reluctance is not a candidate. Trace local dev positions where a loaded carrier already has an active retreat or tribute intent, an observed enemy can reach the normal next tile, and the existing movement path offers a reachable legal shelter without prolonging the retreat. Compare observed exposure before any change. Reuse route safety checks and avoid extra turn-wide search. Predict less gems lost to a second exposed movement, then apply L0–L2. No action or threshold derives from a single historical loss.
