# GX-06: Start a free opening camp on turn one

The default farmer needs group distance fields; its first low-fuel call has no target and can return before taking a useful action. Some maps have harmless gold or gem groups within walking range of spawn. Even one first action could move the first purchase or capture forward without an aggressive opening.

On the first turn only, when there is no enemy in view and fuel can pay for the scan and movement, rank close harmless camps by immediate reward per estimated turn. Use the spawn distance field to reject blocked paths, exclude claimed camps, and hand the chosen camp to the existing camp controller. This changes no subsequent job ranking and does not start a field build. Evaluate round-50 and round-100 economy and fuel as well as paired full-pool wins.
