# GX-37: Avoid sharing a ready area hit

The generic positioning path can treat every adjacent ally as equally risky against a list of area champions. The attack path already compares legal tiles that can hit the same target, so it can use more specific information without splitting the team.

On a melee positioning decision, query ready visible area abilities and test whether a common cast centre can cover both us and a teammate. Penalise that shared exposure only on eligible attack tiles that do not materially raise direct exposure. The second arm includes area dashes and splash. The six-layout, 36-game sample had zero faults or fuel-outs for the control and both active modes. Measure real deaths and damage in the full pool and pressure runs.
