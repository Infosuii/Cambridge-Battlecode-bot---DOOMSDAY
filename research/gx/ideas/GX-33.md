# GX-33: Draft the first two crew jobs with real walking distances

The early replay audit found that first emblems separate wins from losses more reliably than early deposits. The round-one harmless-camp override earned a little more camp gems by round 150 and less gold. A first job should consider crew needs, walking routes around terrain, the opponent's start and the return distance to an altar together.

Use the separate draft meter to compute distance fields, approximate safe crew size from neutral health and outgoing damage, and search short schedules that assign each camp at most once. Pass first and second job rows through draft memory for each feasible body count, including the linked pick. The runtime uses a row only while healthy, free to farm and clear of visible enemies. An occupied allied capture, an expired job, a lack of fuel and an infeasible crew all fall back to the normal bot.

Arms share executable sections: inactive control; first job; second job after the first is complete; first job with a frontier preference. The six-layout, 48-game compatibility sample had zero faults. The largest draft used 2,178,735 fuel of 10,000,000. The first-job arm earned 268.9 gold on average through round 100 versus 292.9 for control in the 12 paired conformance cells. Use full-pool pairs for any conclusion about strength; use earned gold, first-emblem timing, camp gems, deaths and fuel to test the mechanism.
