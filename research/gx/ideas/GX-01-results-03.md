# GX-01: Return travel as a camp tie-break

The full distance penalty and the marginal detour penalty lost strength. Instead, keep the original camp and route scores. Only a healthy solo champion carrying at least 35 gems considers a similarly timed alternative that retains at least 94% of the original camp score and saves at least four steps to the altar. This cannot suppress a high-value contested target early, and it reuses the existing altar field.
