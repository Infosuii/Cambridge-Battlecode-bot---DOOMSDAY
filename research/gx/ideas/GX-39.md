# GX-39: Contest an enemy reward that is about to land

The existing countdown variants used the timer for scoring distant camps and for holding a friendly capture; both regressed. The engine observation probe also saw enemy-owned capture countdowns. An enemy can complete a high-value capture while a healthy supporting champion already stands one legal step away.

Check nearby visible reward tiles only, after the existing immediate kill search. Take one supported contest step when the confirmed enemy timer is at most two rounds, the champion is healthy with a light bag, and the destination has no known neutral or champion burst problem. A narrower arm acts only on substantial rewards or late races. The inactive arm preserves the turn choice. Count changed captures and the effect on both players rather than treating the step as automatically valuable.
