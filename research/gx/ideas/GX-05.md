# GX-05: Aim area spells for camp damage

The existing camp caster aims area spells at a camp's tile. On two rows of wolves, moving the aim by one tile can hit more of the current group. The enemy caster already offsets its aim; the camp caster does not. Geometry also allows some casts to hit a target outside the cast range by aiming one or two tiles closer to the champion.

Keep the original centre as the fallback and choose a visible, in-range tile only if it hits more live camps in the current group. Reject an offset that wakes a neighbouring dangerous group. First try local offsets; then try the range extension separately. The maximum radius and candidate window are bounded, and the scan can return its best valid result early when fuel gets low.
