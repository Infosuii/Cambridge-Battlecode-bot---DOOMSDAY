# GX-22: Spend equal walking progress on a safer bank approach

Large stolen bags are a major part of the pool loss audit. The current step selector chooses the best distance descent, then prefers a diagonal. When two steps have the same distance, the tie can prefer a tile newly inside an enemy's next attack or spell range.

On an active bank route with a substantial bag, compare visible threats and sightings only to break distance ties. Keep path progress, dash and the rest of banking intact. Inspect the carrier's lost-gem and deposit totals as well as pool wins.
