# GX-38: Do not chase into a high personal casualty risk

The champion-perspective contact audit finds that individual health and local support carry information beyond a team-power difference. The compact score flagged 53% of the 117 casualties on layouts and an opponent unseen in training, at a 5% alert rate. That is a forecast of existing outcomes, so a controlled action test is needed.

Use the fixed coefficients only where the normal bot intends to engage. Cancel an out-of-range chase when the individual risk is high, while keeping in-range attacks, safe spells and likely one-turn kills. A second mode also calls the existing escape policy for a substantial bag caught by at least two nearby enemies. All arms use the same executable sections; compare their actions and losses, then run independent opponents and generated layouts if the pool candidate qualifies.
