# GX-21: Validate against distinct local pressure styles

The official pool provides a repeatable reference, but all three opponents share a large part of our architecture. The ladder replay gaps include tighter packs, faster item growth and sustained altar pressure. A candidate can pass the reference pool while leaving those weaknesses untouched.

Build three adversarial benchmarks from the exact control: a grouped stat stacker, an altar lock specialist and a quiet farm-and-deposit racer. Give each a separately reproducible digest and compare the control on the same maps and sides. Before treating a benchmark as informative, verify its expected effect on pack encounters, item timing, locks or banking. These are probes of specific weaknesses; their overall win rate is not a ladder estimate.
