# GX-16: Deposit a bag already at a safe altar

The baseline loss audit identifies 45 of 175 losses where fully recovering both lost and stranded bags could exceed the actual banked deficit. That is an upper bound, but a bag already standing at an unlocked altar has zero travel cost. The default branch checks fights before a winning tribute.

Try the in-place channel early when the current bag would finish the match. Estimate its length from the engine settings and reject dangerous camps, incoming damage, enemy locks and channels that will run past the game. A second mode also accepts a smaller in-place deposit in a late race. The broader long-distance bank policy remains unchanged.
