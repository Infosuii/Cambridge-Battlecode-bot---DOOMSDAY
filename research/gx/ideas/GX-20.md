# GX-20: Remove incoming camp damage sooner

The camp selector mostly orders targets by strength, while the soak estimate assumes it can kill the lowest-health member first. When two hostile camps both attack, the useful quantity is the expected incoming damage removed per turn spent on the kill.

Select by whole basic attacks needed divided by the camp's damage. Keep Varg's splash policy and the large-group path. A second mode applies the same ratio to the continuous team soak estimate. The argument is the two-job exchange: if A precedes B, B deals damage during A's kill time, and vice versa. Prefer the smaller added exposure. Actual range, splash and tank rotation can weaken the approximation, so compare damage and death rates with paired pool outcomes.
