# GX-23: Score the last two draft picks together

The draft network selects one pick at a time from a partial roster. A candidate whose partner makes the pair stronger can look worse alone on the penultimate turn.

On the model's penultimate pick, score each legal pair using the finished-roster input. A second mode selects the first half whose best completion holds up against one possible enemy reply. The search is bounded and runs in the draft budget; known lineups and other picks keep their normal paths. Measure on the pool and generated layouts, and check the exact draft changes before interpreting results.
