# GX-32: Respond locally to changed camp safety

The camp deficit was at least 20% in 84 of 175 x3mf pool losses. Test two local responses: avoid a recent unsupported death site when a comparable alternative exists; increase the value of a nearby camp when a fresh count finds an ally advantage and the camp can be taken before that information goes stale. Recent support can release the death-site penalty.

The first variant stores one short-lived site. The second uses a fuel-gated periodic count and short age bound rather than evaluating numbers inside every camp comparison. Measure neutral captures, deaths and fuel by phase, then overall paired outcomes.
