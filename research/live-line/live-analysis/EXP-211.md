# EXP-211 — Native clear/capture attribution before choosing a production fix

EXP208 confirms lower final camp production and clear count, but gems per clear cannot distinguish reward mix, splitting or theft. The team's independent base02 native audit found only1–4% clear theft; do not assume that transfers to the fresh ladder cohort. Freeze the following exact-event attribution before calculating it, unchanged across discovery,control,confirmation.

Sort campClear and campGroup gemCollection entries by native input/ordinal. Batch each capture by(groupID,input); assert a single capturing team and sum all champion shares, including zero-gem packets. Attribute to the most recent preceding clear of that group. Record time-to-capture, source team, captured total, participants, and each champion share. Record unmatched packets and clears without a later capture; never silently treat either as a successful capture. Independent sum of packet gems must equal each team's resource ledger camp production, and all clear counts must agree. A clear's reward taken by the enemy is observed attribution, not proof of a decision error.

Report final and R150/R300/R800 acquired camp gems, captures, split-packet frequency, mean gem value per packet, own-clear enemy-capture rate, uncaptured clears and delay distributions. Group-correlated,outcome-selected,inferred source identity; no causal ceiling or strength claim. Unlike the old snapshot inference, this uses native target IDs and actual gem shares. Do not tune a policy on confirmation identities. Use discovery mechanisms only to propose a controlled own-view reproduction and new varied-opponent comparison.
