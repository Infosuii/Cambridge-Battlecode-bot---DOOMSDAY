# Discovery 6 experiment cards

## EXP-074 — Check the one-action camp finish before a shopping recall

Gift Wrapping led in early earned gold and trailed in gems. Test EXP-050's narrower opportunity at the actual recall decision, using a visible one-hit camp in the committed group, safe health and no nearby enemies. Require that the group reward can be collected from the current tile and confirm the basic attack and resulting payout in the native replay. Require two maps and two opponents before a release. Preserve the shop destination.

## EXP-075 — Refresh a ward that just lost sight of the active camp

The winner placed more wards in all eight reserved games. Use exact own-view traces to count a ward expiry followed by a visible camp approach losing sight of a chosen attack tile. Probe an existing ward charge at a previously valid tile, with the next attack demonstrably restored. Require the effect on two dev maps with no additional world refresh. Whole-game ward count alone is insufficient.

## EXP-076 — Preserve the first profitable camp behind a blocked approach

Split lost 89 collected gems before proximity. Probe independently reachable targets where a safe route's first move is refused by a friendly unit while a same-distance first move exists and the current job can be retained. The early collection claim requires earlier native capture on two maps and both opponents, with identical draft. Avoid a rule based on total visible camps.

## EXP-077 — Measure retreat opportunities before a no-action death

On Shortcut the same-role Vendetta comparison was alive versus dead with a 56-gem team deficit. Identify the earlier own-view turn when the dying champion first had a visible threat and a legal retreat tile. Require a matched replay of the saved step against at least two varied opponents. Keep a target-specific attack if it still resolves before the retreat. Do not infer a missing retreat from the death or the R150 state.

## EXP-078 — Distinguish camp value from incidental gold at the route decision

In Gift Wrapping the R150 gold and gem leads disagreed. Probe whether the active score picks a job with more estimated gold but less directly visible gem yield than an equal-time safe job. Compare native captures and later game wins. Require the choice to exist on multiple dev maps with both invader and top-12 proxy opponents. Keep any rule inactive while the replay only shows correlation.
