# EXP-029 — Match the first camp commitment to nearby roles

[Discovery 4](discovery4/DISCOVERY.md) measures more workers together among winners and a positive camp-gold gap, with similar capture counts. Probe two or more living allies independently committing to low-value groups while a nearby higher-value camp already has a ready tank and is viable only with another attacker. Use observed paths, current claims and existing damage/soak estimates. Limit the proposed commitment to one initial job and preserve the solo route if there is no viable match. This is a first-job assignment, separate from the rejected ongoing pack movement. Predict an earlier first team clear and fewer opening approach turns, and require multiple maps and both varied opponents before designing the matching rule.
