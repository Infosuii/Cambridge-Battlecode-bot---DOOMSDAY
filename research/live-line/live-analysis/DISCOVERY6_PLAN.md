# Discovery 6 — Early clears, contact and carry losses

Freeze 24 distinct ranked losses from the preceding 48 hours to teams above DOOMSDAY or among the named rivals, with a fixed hash order, four games per opponent, and every third game reserved. Exclude the five previous samples and all direct rival replay cells. Run the same replay metrics and the earlier frozen two-seed subsystem ablations for the unchanged v92 controls.

Compare early earned and inferred camp gold, gems collected, deposits, first contact and deaths with no action. Distinguish deficits before first enemy contact from deficits that open after it. Treat visible camp count and no-action deaths as observations, with no inference about unrecorded decisions. A proposed new decision needs an own-view counterfactual and a direction repeated in at least five of eight reserved games.
