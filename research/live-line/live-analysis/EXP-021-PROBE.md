# EXP-021 carrier opportunity probe

Observe the actual successful downhill movement chosen by v92 while banking or walking home. Restrict the probe to bags of at least 30 gems, the existing three-round channel scale. Exclude moves preceded by a dash. The current tile and successful destination must both lie within visible enemy attack range plus one step. A candidate alternate must be another unblocked adjacent tile at the same distance to the goal, outside that threat band and the awake camp mask.

Use 16 frozen conformance maps, both sides against v92 and x3inv. The charged probe leaves choices unchanged and counts the counterfactual after the normal step succeeds. Record at least two affected dev maps and zero faults before designing a candidate. A geometrically eligible alternate is an opportunity, not proof the engine would accept the step or that it prevents a death.
