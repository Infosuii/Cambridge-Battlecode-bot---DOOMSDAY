# EXP-033 — Require a gem-preserving opening reward improvement

EXP-030's independent 64 development pairs gained 335 inferred camp gold by R150 and lost 104 camp gems. Preserve the completion-window eligibility and the same 25% modeled improvement. Add a separate objective constraint: the alternative must forecast at least as many camp gems as the best comparable completion-window sequence from the existing route's first job. Count nominal group gems only when the modeled job completes within the window. Keep the original choice when the strongest alternative fails the gem requirement. This rule addresses the development trade-off and does not use the identity or outcome of any official-pool case.

Freeze the same 64 charged development cells, require actual decision opportunities on two maps against both opponents and zero faults. If it qualifies, the exact-release behavior gate requires increases in aggregate inferred camp gold and nondecreasing aggregate camp gems through R150, plus an improvement on at least two maps. Use the original L0–L2 and ladder gates.
