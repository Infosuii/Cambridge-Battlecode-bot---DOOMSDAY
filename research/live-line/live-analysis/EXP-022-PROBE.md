# EXP-022 opportunity probe

Existing v92 chooses the poorer ally for a reward in both camp scoring and final capture handling. A Vesper preference can change that decision even when both bodies are already on the region. This probe measures that narrower case on the 16 frozen conformance maps, both sides against v92 and x3inv. It keeps the old answer and logs the proposed answer only when they differ. No movement toward an unclaimed reward is credited.

Before round 300, when the deciding champion is on the reward, prefer a Vesper already standing there at or above 65% health. Require another body on the reward and leave directed-job choices intact. Vesper keeps the claim and another champion yields. The 65% health boundary is the existing safe shop-deferral threshold. Use the same ownership predicate at scoring and final handling to avoid inconsistent answers.

Require decision differences on at least two independent dev maps, complete replay capture and zero faults before a release candidate. Count unique rounds, champions and groups separately from duplicate scoring calls. The diagnostic is charged and makes no strength claim. Freeze any draft change separately. Holdout and selection stay sealed.
