# EXP-277 — Co-design role workload and power conversion

Fresh fixed views against an iq too high show our Marcella maxHP1200atR150/200 versus rival1400/1700, while gold, position and carried gems also differ. This is observable power separation, not proof of a particular item purchase or bad shopping: growth upgrades, inventory, income timing and trips must be distinguished. Generic banking/item/solo-owner switches already failed.

Audit native income/spending and available own-view growth/inventory against a role's clear-and-survive workload. Link a purchase/return opportunity to legal current resources and actual travel/channel cost; do not infer hidden rival intent. Use same-group capability controls from274and native task outcomes from275, with all failures/censoring retained.

Only then freeze a small coherent draft/play/inventory factorial, e.g. a durable splash farmer versus the incumbent workload, and tune short ES trials on wins averaged across varied development families/opponents with own-score noise. Preserve matchup/style leave-one-out checks and use fresh noise for the chosen package. No forced hero from farm-gem totals, map lookup, income optimisation, long neural training or relaxed gate. This is a new structural role package, not a re-labelled old Stellar/AOE patch.
