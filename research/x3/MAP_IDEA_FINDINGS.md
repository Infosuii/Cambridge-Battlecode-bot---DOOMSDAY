# The map idea: findings (3 Nov 2026)

What was tried, what was measured, what works, what does not, and where to pick it up. Everything here is on branch
`build/z-plan`. Detailed working notes: `research/x3/losses48/PLAYBOOK.md`.

## 1. The idea

Not one universal bot: a **portfolio**. At game start the bot reads the map, puts it in a **category**, and plays the
**strategy profile** that is best for that category. Categories are rules on map features, so unseen maps are covered.
Strategies are taken from what the teams that beat us actually did. Every choice is judged only on the **ladder**
(paired match groups against real teams), never on self-play.

## 3. Evidence: what the teams that beat us do

898 distinct losses to teams rated >= 2000 in the 48 h to 3 Nov 15:44 UTC (`tools/ladder/losses_recent.py`; summaries
`data/ladder/losses48/summaries`, replays local only). Analyses: `tools/ladder/summary_profile.py`, `archetypes.py`,
`tools/replay/winner_profile.mjs`, `tools/ladder/loss_study.py`, `research/x3/losses48/replay_compare.py`.
Results: `research/x3/losses48/ARCHETYPES.md`, `REPLAY_FINDINGS.md`.

- **Draft (largest, most consistent difference):** the winners pick **Vesper Vale in 66-84%** of games in every
  category; we pick her in 0-53% (C1 0%, C3 24%, C4 16%, C5 12%). We always pick Vendetta (100%) and lean on Varg
  (C5 87% vs their 14%) and Cassi + Issac (46-80% vs 15-38%). They also pick Ryth and Tavi more. v92 drafts nearly
  fixed lineups.
- **Vision:** they place 2-3x our wards in every category.
- **Banking:** first deposit 50-290 rounds earlier, 1.4-2.9x more deposits.
- **Items:** Bruiser 3-10 shards by round 300 (us 0); Treasure opening ~30% (us 0%).
- **Movement:** grouped 68% vs 57% early; 1.72 vs 1.44 champions per camp; 2.1 vs 1.7 allies at first contact;
  steadily more time on our half (own half 0.83 -> 0.70 over the game, ours 0.86 -> 0.83).
- **Late game:** in C1 / C4 we collapse late (C1 clears after round 800 9.8 vs 22.5; C4 7.3 vs 23.4).
- **Nine archetypes** (k-means, overlapping): our-half killers 244, Treasure openers 163, raiders 126, contested
  farmers 122, pacifist racers 80, Bruiser openers 64, hoarders 54, early brawlers 32, Power invaders 13. Teams have
  house styles (top archetype share 22-60%).

## 5. Lessons

1. **In-game strategy layers on top of v92 do not beat v92.** Knob multipliers and borrowed behaviours (even when the
   behaviour matches the winners, as pack play did) unbalance a bot whose settings were tuned together. The winners'
   grouping works because their whole game supports it.
2. **Fuel is part of every strategy.** A one-time analysis on round 1 caused 30 fuel-outs in 28 games (v92: 6) and
   biased profiles 1-4; a world refresh per ward cost about 10 games. Measure fuel and fuel-out rounds
   (`fuelExhausted` events in summaries) before any ladder test.
3. **Check behaviour before results** (`behaviour_check.py`): it separates "the idea is wrong" from "the code does
   not do the idea".
4. **Pin opponents** (`--source-group`) or paired arms drift apart as teams upload new bots.
5. **The ladder is the only judge.** Self-play and per-cell tables were memorised chaos.

## 6. Where to pick it up

1. **Draft by category** (the untested lever, and the channel the organisers designed the maps for): per category, a
   lineup built from what the winners pick there (Vesper Vale; Ryth / Tavi where they use them), chosen at draft time
   from map features; test on the pinned duel against v92 and read per category. Caution: our past Vesper games won
   about 50%, so her item build and fight logic may need work.
2. **Read the remaining clean profile results** (plan c) per category; assign a profile only where it beats v92 on
   that category's maps by a clear margin; otherwise keep v92's play.
3. **Second axis, opponent style:** detect raiders / our-half killers / pacifists / stat openers from early sightings
   and switch to a counter profile mid-game.
