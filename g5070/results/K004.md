Adding secure and burst-hold together to P3 changes wins by -6. This two-switch contrast cannot attribute the effect to either feature or establish their interaction. Single-addition ablations are needed before strategic rejection.

Execution order is explicit in `bots/x1/src/bot.cpp`: plan hook at ~6956 precedes anti-feed at ~6993. Plan 4 sets territory=0 and aggression x0.65; triggered anti-feed then sets the same territory, multiplies aggression x0.6, raises retreat HP by .15 (cap .95), and danger TTD x1.3. Combined aggression is x0.39. This establishes duplicated territory control and compounded caution. It does not prove that compounding caused a measured loss.

Secure's target TTK estimate at ~3222 counts allies up to attack range +2, whereas its ~3247 comment describes a kill this round. That reach approximation is inherited from baseline targeting. It can make the secure bonus optimistic about allies who still need to move; the current evidence is implementation uncertainty, not a confirmed bug. Smallest diagnostic: expose/log actual ready in-range contributing damage and compare secure bonus decisions on changed loss cases; retest a range/ready-aware correction behind a switch with the same full gate.
