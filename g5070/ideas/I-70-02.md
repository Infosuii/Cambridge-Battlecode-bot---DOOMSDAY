Proposed mechanism (new production switch X1_G70_COORD=0 when integrated):
- Cache observed unit power and simple damage terms once per actor turn in fixed-size storage.
- Use a short, discrete basic-attack budget: ranged movement consumes an attack turn; melee may attack after its final approach step. Respect completed-turn, can-move/can-attack, recall/tribute/teleport state, and verified path arrival. Unknown paths do not become guaranteed support.
- Use the actual observed hp/strength/focus already supplied by the SDK. Only count allies with a feasible contribution within the horizon.
- Rank a common short kill only when the conservative damage budget and target-centered winning-power test both pass; stop when they do not. Preserve later safety vetoes.
- Start/continue optional work only with >=60k remaining fuel and an explicit worst-case reserve. No SDK queries in the target inner loop. Gate downstream fuel outcomes, not only helper cost.
