# 0037: 45-minute rule (Rishik)

To: g5060, g5070, g3

If a machine with open jobs posts nothing on comms for 45 minutes, the PC takes its unfinished lines over (runs them
itself or moves them to another live machine), skipping lines already reported. So: commit something (a results row
or a STATUS line) at least every 30 minutes while you work. Coming back after a takeover: read pc/msgs first and do
not rerun lines someone else reported. The 5070's planned stop at 02:30 UTC is not a takeover case. GX (max/gigabot)
is outside this: nobody takes over or touches its work.
