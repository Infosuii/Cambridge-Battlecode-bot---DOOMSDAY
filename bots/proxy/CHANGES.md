# proxy (2026-10-27): a local stand-in for the ladder's top teams

Not a ladder candidate and never submitted. It is the opponent `tools/proxy/local_duel.py` plays our versions against,
so that a change can be judged against something that plays like the top teams instead of against our own lineage.
Method, data and validation: `research/proxy/README.md`.

Base: `bots/v66` (= v65, platform 65, with the v66 switches), with `V66_LEAVE 0` (v65's fight verdicts) and
`V66_ITEMS 1` (the hand-written engine-2 item table) for heroes the imitated team never played.

| Switch | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `PROXY` | 1 | Enables everything below. |
| `PROXY_TEAM` | -1 | Row of `src/proxy_tables.h` to imitate: -1 = all top teams pooled; 0-11 = one team (the duel plan's opponents sorted by name; `local_duel.py --per-team` sets it from the team's name). |
| `PROXY_DRAFT` | 1 | Draft the team's lineup for this map: the most common pick sequence it used there (map identified by a hash of size, draft size and camp layout, `draft.cpp proxy_map_key`); maps without data use its overall pick order. |
| `PROXY_ITEMS` | 1 | Buy the team's purchase milestones for each hero: its most common first four, then its stat stacks in its proportion. |
| `PROXY_PERMA_RECALL` | 1 | Teams flagged in the table (Mite, NEETCoders, Trzy Kwaterki, willow best girl, print dont int) end every turn by starting a recall and cancel it at the start of the next, as they do in more than 85% of their turns. |
| `PROXY_PRINT_MAPID` | 0 | Print the map key at draft time (used once by `compile_proxy.py mapkeys`). |

`src/proxy_tables.h` is generated by `tools/proxy/compile_proxy.py tables` from a meta crawl and the duel games;
`research/proxy/TABLES.md` lists what is in it.

Fight knobs fitted to the top teams' play against v65 (`tools/proxy/fit_style.py`, run f1), set in `src/common.h`:
`APEX_COHESION 0.5f` (was 0.05), `APEX_RETREAT_HP 0.25f` (both lines; was 0.1288), `NOVA_TD_LIFE1 15.0f` (was 25),
`NOVA_FIGHT2 0` (unchanged). With them v65 beats the proxy by +250 instead of +300 (top teams beat v65 by 386).
